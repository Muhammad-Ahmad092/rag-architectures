Frequently asked questions

What is the warranty on Nimbus robots?

All Nimbus X1 and S2 robots include a 3-year limited hardware warranty. Batteries are covered for 2 years.

What temperatures can the Nimbus X1 operate in?

The X1 operates between -10 °C and 45 °C, which makes it suitable for chilled warehouses but not deep-freeze storage.

When is customer support available?

Standard support runs Monday to Friday, 07:00–19:00 Pacific Time. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support.

How do firmware updates work?

NimbusOS updates are staged: 5% of a fleet first, then the rest after 24 hours without critical alerts.
